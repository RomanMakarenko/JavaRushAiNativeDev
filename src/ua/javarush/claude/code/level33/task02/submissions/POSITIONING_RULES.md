# AI-Assisted Engineering Workflow

## Core statement
I own the engineering work and final decisions; AI provides supportive assistance with exploration, drafting, and iteration.

## Allowed wording
- AI-assisted development with developer-led ownership.
- I use AI to explore options, draft implementation details, and accelerate iteration.
- I define the task, review the output, run tests, and make the final decision.
- AI supports my workflow; responsibility for the result remains with me.
- I validate AI-generated suggestions against the requirements and codebase.

## Unsafe wording
- AI built the project for me.
- I blindly accepted the AI-generated code.
- The AI is responsible for the implementation or outcome.
- I have experience that I gained only through AI-generated claims.
- AI made all the engineering decisions.

## Ownership signals
- **Task spec:** I define the problem, scope, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
- **Review:** I inspect the proposed changes and verify that they fit the requirements and codebase.
- **Tests:** I run or add relevant tests and investigate failures before accepting the result.
- **Final decision:** I decide what to keep, revise, or reject and remain accountable for the shipped work.

## Short bio example
I am a developer who uses AI as a supportive tool for exploration, drafting, and iteration. I define the work, review the implementation, run tests, and make the final engineering decisions. My profile describes only experience I can personally substantiate.
